# GraxCare Ready — Conversion Reset

Date: 2026-08-17

## Objective
Stop cosmetic iteration. Optimize GraxCare Ready for understanding, discoverability, clicks, and purchases.

## Core product definition
GraxCare Ready shows you what to do when a family member has a health, medication, hospital, caregiver, or safety problem.

## Core operating message
Family info. Real-life situations. Clear next steps.

Choose the person. Choose what happened. Follow the steps.

Start with a name. Add details later.

## What GraxCare is NOT
- Not a complete medical record.
- Not medical advice.
- Not a replacement for emergency services.
- Not a contact list.

## What a family profile is
A profile is the person the user may need to help: self, spouse, parent, child, or another family member. It can hold only the essential information useful in a real situation: medications, allergies, doctor/clinic, pharmacy, hospital, insurance, primary contact, and support needs.

## Minimum setup promise
Only name + relationship are required to create a family member. Other details can be added later when useful.

## Problem-Language Discovery Map
Use natural problem language first. Do not expect the customer to know the category name.

1. I can’t reach them
   - can't reach my mom what do I do
   - my dad isn't answering his phone
   - what should I do if I can't contact my elderly parent

2. We need urgent care
   - my mom needs urgent care what do I need
   - what should I take with me to urgent care for my dad
   - elderly parent suddenly needs medical help

3. We are going to the hospital
   - what do I bring to the hospital for my mom
   - dad is going to the hospital what do I need
   - hospital checklist for elderly parent

4. Back from the hospital
   - mom just came home from hospital what do I do now
   - how do I organize discharge instructions for my parent
   - what should I keep track of after hospital discharge

5. Medication changed
   - my mom's meds changed how do I keep track
   - how to organize medications for elderly parent
   - dad has new medication what should I update

6. I need help from family
   - I need help taking care of my mom
   - how do I get family to help with dad
   - how do we divide caregiving tasks

7. Caregiver unavailable
   - mom's caregiver canceled what do I do
   - caregiver didn't show up for my parent
   - backup plan when caregiver is unavailable

8. Suspicious call or message
   - my elderly mom got a scam call what should I do
   - dad clicked a suspicious message
   - how do I protect my parent from scam calls

9. Phone or alert device problem
   - mom's phone isn't working and she lives alone
   - medical alert device not working what do I do
   - elderly parent can't use phone right now

10. Travel or away from home
   - traveling with elderly parent medications what do I need
   - what medical info should I carry for my mom when traveling
   - checklist for traveling with aging parent

11. Follow-up appointment
   - what should I ask at my mom's follow up appointment
   - how do I prepare for dad's doctor appointment
   - what should I bring to a follow up visit for my parent

12. Information needs updating
   - how do I keep my mom's medical info organized
   - where do I keep dad's medications doctors and insurance info
   - simple way to organize family health information

## Conversion messaging hierarchy
### 1. What is it?
Know what to do when a family member needs help.

### 2. How does it work?
Choose the person. Choose what happened. Follow the steps.

### 3. Why is the person list useful?
Each profile keeps that person's essential information ready when a situation happens.

### 4. Is setup difficult?
No. Start with a name. Add details later.

### 5. What problems does it cover?
12 real-life situations involving health, medications, hospital visits, caregivers, communication, safety, travel, and follow-up.

- Show a specific problem.
- Show the 3-step workflow.
- Show a concrete benefit.

Primary assets must do all three.

## Shopify / AI discovery rule
Product title, first description lines, image alt text, FAQ, and captions should use both:
- clear product language, and
- natural problem-language phrases customers may use before they know GraxCare exists.

Avoid abstract phrases such as "family care system" unless immediately explained with a concrete function.
